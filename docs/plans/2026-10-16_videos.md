# Videos: transcribed and summarised (plan, 2026-10-16)

The operator: "the next thing to build is these videos; I want the transcript summary for both YouTube and the
Twitter ones". Built 2026-10-16 (ARCHITECTURE "Long videos"), on the answers below.

## What is there
- One morning's read (2026-10-16 06:54, 405 posts in the brief) carried 90 X videos (449 minutes in all; 19 over
  2 minutes, 6 over 10) and 5 YouTube links. The 15 Oct 11:15 read: 33 videos (299 minutes), 1 YouTube link.
  Counts are over every page read, so a few posts older than the last brief are in them.
- The feed already carries each X video's `video_info`: its length and its mp4 files on video.twimg.com.
- On this laptop: yt-dlp, ffmpeg, and Whisper large-v3 on MLX (the Telegram voice notes' setup,
  `~/.venvs/telegram`). Transcribing runs locally, at no cost.

## The design
1. **Which**: the videos of the posts the brief holds (the post's own, or what it reposts or quotes at any depth),
   and YouTube links in those posts.
2. **Fetch the audio**, outside the browser, direct (public files, like the Sites tab's reads): an X video's
   smallest mp4 from video.twimg.com, a YouTube video's audio through yt-dlp. Rule 1 and 2 stay as they are: the
   browser opens nothing more; this is a plain download of a file the feed already named.
3. **Transcribe** with Whisper large-v3, the whole video, kept in the data dir, one file per video, with times.
   A video with no speech (music, a chart scrolling) gets no transcript and is marked so.
4. **The writer** gets each transcript whole, like an Article's text (`video.transcript`), and is told what it is
   (a machine transcript: names and tickers may be misheard). A video that argues a claim is a thesis; videos lead
   the theses with Articles, marked "Video". A news clip goes in its story.
5. **On the page**: the post shows its video's length and a link to the transcript; search reaches the words.
6. **Old briefs**: `xbrief videos <raw>` does the same for a saved run, then rebriefs it.

## Answers so far (2026-10-16)
Only videos of 10 minutes or longer. The fastest way, not downloading a video and running Whisper on it. YouTube's
own captions.

## Measured (2026-10-16)
- **X carries its own captions**: a video's stream index (its .m3u8, named in the feed) lists an "en
  (auto-generated)" subtitle track, one WebVTT file with word timings, fetched in 0.2–0.3 s, public, no login.
  3 of this morning's 6 long videos had one; the other 3 (55, 60 and 91 minutes) had none.
- **X's captions against Whisper large-v3** (MLX, on the video's audio-only track, 64 kbps):

  | video | length | X words | Whisper words | same words | Whisper time | audio fetch |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Coin Center dinner speech | 20 min | 2,383 | 2,373 | 97.5% | 75 s | 16 s |
  | Threadguy show (interview) | 74 min | 17,314 | 17,453 | 95.3% | 381 s | — |

  Where they differ, neither wins: Whisper got Chaincode, Consovoy, Neeraj, David Senra, perp, "broker act"; X got
  FinCEN, Wyden, Perkins Coie, Ashurst, NFTs, "how memetic", and a sung intro Whisper dropped. Most of the rest is
  fillers, repeats and number style ("a hundred" / "100"). X's captions are as good as Whisper here.
- **YouTube's captions could not be fetched from here**: yt-dlp (2026.08.19) got HTTP 429 twice on the caption file
  (only auto-captions exist for the video tried), and the transcript panel did not open in Chrome. The audio itself
  came in 4 s.

Whisper here runs 12–16 times faster than the audio plays: this morning's three long videos without captions
(3.4 hours) would take about 15 minutes, on the audio track only (about 30 MB an hour), never the video.

## Decided
- An uncaptioned long X video: Whisper on its audio track (operator, 2026-10-16).
- YouTube: Whisper on its audio (operator, 2026-10-16).
