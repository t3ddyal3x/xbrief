# The morning brief before the operator wakes, caught up when they do (2026-10-20, plan, not built)

Operator: "the biggest change we can make is running this before i wake up and then running it again to get the
latest updates that we didn't get before". The Mac stays awake overnight, plugged in. They wake around 07:00. The
catch-up is folded into the morning's one brief, not a second brief.

## Why it works
A run reads from where the last one stopped, and videos are kept by key, so the early run carries the cost (the
night's read, the videos, the writer over ~400 posts: 75 min on 10-20, about 50 at normal priority) and a run at
07:00 reads only what came after it: on 10-15 such a run (1 page, 73 posts) took 2 min.

## The plan
1. **Early run**: the schedule (already built, off) turned on with the morning window moved to about 05:00–05:45.
2. **Catch-up at wake**: Brief now, or a second morning slot at ~06:45 (open question 2).
3. **Folded**: the catch-up does not make a brief of its own; it updates the morning's (open question 1).

## Open questions
1. How the catch-up folds in:
   a. Update the stories (recommended): the writer gets the morning's stories and only the new posts, and returns
      the stories the new posts join (their posts, a revised gist) and new stories. Short answer, so minutes. Code
      merges them into the morning's brief and stores it under the same name. Exact: every post is placed by the writer.
   b. Write the whole brief again over both reads' posts: simplest, exact, but the writer over ~500 posts again
      (~10 min) every catch-up.
   c. One page, two parts: "Since 05:40" above "Overnight". Two writers' stories may tell the same story twice.
2. Catch-up by hand (Brief now) or on the schedule at ~06:45.
3. Untested: whether X's feed loads in the parked Brave window while the display sleeps (displaysleep is 60 min, so
   at 05:00 it is asleep). A hidden page stops drawing (CLAUDE.md rule 6). Test with `bin/tests --browser` while the
   display sleeps, or by one early run, before relying on it.
